Crosses & Clippers was publicly featured by the Alberton Record in July 2025. The report documented Davian Benjamin's new barbering business in Eden Park and support he received from entrepreneur Sheldon Tatchell and FXDE.

The story is part of the brand's history. The next chapter is the work: building a respected local barbering business, one client and one cut at a time.
