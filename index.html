MY STORY

BUILT WITH
PURPOSE.

Crosses & Clippers grew from a personal journey into a business built around rebuilding, learning, barbering and giving people a reason to believe in what comes next.

THE SUPPORT THAT HELPED MOVE THE BUSINESS FORWARD

The Alberton Record reported in July 2025 that Davian Benjamin, an upcoming barbershop entrepreneur in Eden Park, reached out to businessman Sheldon Tatchell, founder of Legends Barber, for support.

The report says Sheldon and his team supplied professional barbershop equipment and guidance on growing the business. It also highlights the support and hope the equipment represented while Davian rebuilt his life and business.
