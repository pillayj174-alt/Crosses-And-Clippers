FEATURED BY ALBERTON RECORD • 11 JULY 2025

FROM REBUILDING TO BUILDING A BUSINESS

The Alberton Record reported that Eden Park barber and Crosses & Clippers founder Davian Benjamin reached out to businessman Sheldon Tatchell, founder of Legends Barber, for support as he worked to grow his business.

The article says Sheldon and his team supplied professional barbershop equipment and guidance on how to scale the business. It also records Davian's gratitude for the support and the hope it brought to his community.

The story further highlights Benjamin's recovery and determination: the article reported that he had overcome a 12-year drug addiction and had been sober and clean for one year and six months as of June 2025.

It also describes Tatchell's wider give-back work through Fxde, including its model of donating one clipper to a barber in need for every ten clippers sold.
